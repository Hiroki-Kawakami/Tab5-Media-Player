# c_alac, which cannot be opened and is skipped, then d_pcm — as `--log`
# shows in audio_out's open lines; a touch ends the music with the pictures.

# is opened once however long it plays. The picker opens at the source chosen
# before, or at the pictures when there is none; its storage list has no way
# back, and Cancel leaves the source as it was. The transition is set to Fade first so the
# rows sit where the script expects, and music is switched off at the end;
# the chosen source stays in NVS.
